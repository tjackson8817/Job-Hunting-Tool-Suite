Job Hunting Tool Suite

A set of standalone tools that turn a name-and-industry hunch into researched companies, real postings, tailored applications, drafted outreach, grounded interview prep, and an actual negotiation plan — each one works on its own, and together they cover a full job search.

How they fit together

Steps 1 and 2 run in that order — the Job Posting Finder needs a priority-ranked shortlist that only exists after the Prompt Builder has already run. Steps 3a and 3b have no fixed order: network first and tailor your resume once a real opening exists, or find a posting first and tailor immediately, then follow up with outreach. Both are legitimate — the tools don't force either sequence. Step 4 comes after Step 3 actually lands an interview, whichever path got you there — pick whichever of its three modes matches the interview you're prepping for, and afterward the specifics you discuss become real Context Notes for a follow-up message back in Step 3b's Outreach Message Builder. Step 5 comes after Step 4 actually produces a real offer — it's the direct sequel to the salary-range coaching already built into Step 4's Recruiter Screen mode, for once a real number is on the table.

Also useful — outside the job-search funnel

These two aren't steps in the funnel above. One helps you stay visible on LinkedIn whether or not you're actively job hunting; the other helps you screen suspicious recruiter outreach the moment it lands in your inbox.
