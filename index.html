Job Hunting Tool Suite

Four standalone tools that turn a name-and-industry hunch into researched companies, real postings, tailored applications, and drafted outreach — each one works on its own, and together they cover a full job search.

STEP 1 · RESEARCH

Target Company Prompt Builder

Researches and ranks companies for your industry, purpose, or NAICS code — builds a formatted Excel tracker with a computed Suggested Priority Rank, so you know where to focus first.

STEP 2 · VERIFY

Job Posting Finder

Checks who's actively hiring, right now — real, current job postings with real URLs, not company research. Built to report fewer results honestly rather than pad the list.

STEP 3 · APPLY OR CONNECT

Resume & Cover Letter Tailoring

Tailors your actual resume and cover letter to a specific posting — keyword/gap analysis, bullet rewrites, a traditional letter or a two-column Qualifications Match Letter. Never invents a qualification you don't have.

STEP 3 · APPLY OR CONNECT

Outreach Message Builder

Drafts the actual messages you send — tailored per company based on your real connection type, not one generic template. Never invents a relationship that doesn't exist.

How they fit together

Steps 1 and 2 run in that order — the Job Posting Finder needs a priority-ranked shortlist that only exists after the Prompt Builder has already run. Steps 3 have no fixed order: network first and tailor your resume once a real opening exists, or find a posting first and tailor immediately, then follow up with outreach. Both are legitimate — the tools don't force either sequence.
