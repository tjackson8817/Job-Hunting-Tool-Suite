Job Posting Finder

Companion tool — turns a shortlist of companies and/or titles into a prompt that finds real, current job postings.

This is the companion to the Target Company Prompt Builder, not a replacement. That tool researches companies and ranks them (Suggested Priority Rank). This tool takes a shortlist — ideally your top few by that rank — and asks Claude to actually search for live job postings. Best practice: run the company research first, then paste your highest-priority companies and their Suggested Job Title Keywords in here.
1 · Paste from your tracker optional

Two ways to build your list, and you can use either one alone or both together: bulk paste rows straight from your tracker below, or skip this box and just type companies/titles by hand in Section 2. Parsed rows land in an editable list either way, so you can fix or add to anything before generating your prompt.

Keeps each company's own tailored title instead of blending everything into one generic list — Dragos still searches "VP, Professional Services" while KPMG searches "Partner, Cyber Security Services," not a mix of both.

Didn't paste anything above? Click this to add a row from scratch — no bulk paste required.

2 · What to search for

Fill in at least one — company names alone, job titles/keywords alone, parsed rows above, or any combination.

Tip: paste your top few companies by Suggested Priority Rank rather than an entire tracker — this runs live search per company, so a shortlist keeps results focused and keeps the request from getting too large. (These search with whatever title logic you set below, applied to every company here. If you want each company matched to its own specific title instead, use the bulk paste box in Section 1.)

Leave blank to search broadly across each company's open roles instead of specific titles.

Used as a real boolean search query on LinkedIn, Indeed, and Google, which all support AND/OR/quotes natively. Sources without boolean search get the intent interpreted manually.

3 · Scope

Guidance, not a hard cap. Fewer per combination usually means better-verified results — see the honesty note below.

4 · Output

Green = posted in the last few days, yellow = getting older, red = near the edge of your recency window — a quick visual read on freshness.

Why this matters: a fabricated job posting is worse than no posting at all — it wastes your time chasing something that doesn't exist. This tool's generated prompt explicitly tells Claude to only report postings it actually finds via live search with a real, working URL, and to report fewer results rather than pad the list. That instruction is built in below and can't be turned off.
